OEDI S3 bucket (`s3://oedi-data-lake/pvdaq/`).

> NREL. (2021). *Photovoltaic Data Acquisition (PVDAQ) Public Datasets* [data set].
> https://dx.doi.org/10.25984/1846021

OEDI lists the authors as Chris Deline, Kirsten Perry, Michael Deceglie, Matthew Muller,
William Sekulic and Dirk Jordan.

## Derived files

Small derived tables you choose to commit (for example `year_scorecard.csv`) are derived from
PVDAQ data. If you publish them, keep this attribution next to them and state that the data was
modified (filtered, aggregated, flagged).

## Scope

Only use **public** PVDAQ systems. OEDI states that data from the PV Fleets Initiative is covered
by confidentiality agreements and is not shared through PVDAQ unless the owner authorizes it.
